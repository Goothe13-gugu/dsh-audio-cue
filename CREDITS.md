## Default cues

`assets/loop.ogg`, `assets/loop.mp3`, and `assets/needs-you.mp3` are synthesized
placeholders generated with `ffmpeg`; the exact commands are in `CHANGELOG.md`.
They are original to this package and covered by its MIT license.

## Everything else

No other third-party media is bundled. The plugin ships no fonts, icons, or
images: the toggle and the panel are built from text and CSS.
